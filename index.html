    Population Simulation Model

    Compare population growth and decay across multiple years.

    Population simulator

    Explore how a population changes over time.

    Enter an initial population, a rate, and the number of years. The model calculates unconstrained exponential growth and decay, plus constrained comparison models.

    Simulation settings

    The carrying capacity limits the constrained-growth model. The constrained-decay comparison uses 90% of the entered decay rate.
